⚠️ Allergy Reference
Anaphylactic reaction to soy protein. Hospital-level reaction, twice confirmed.
Always check supplement labels for: soy lecithin, soya, soy protein, soybean oil
Generally tolerated: Fermented soy — soy sauce, miso, tempeh (fermentation breaks down the allergenic proteins)
Unpredictable: "May contain traces of soy" products — reactions have occurred inconsistently. Treat with caution.
